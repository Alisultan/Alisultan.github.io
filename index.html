Real-time motor control

An MT6701 magnetic encoder feeds shaft position into SimpleFOC. A dedicated FreeRTOS task handles the motor loop while Bluetooth and logging run separately.

Calibration & protection

The firmware checks pole pairs and sensor direction, measures the electrical zero, and compensates for encoder nonlinearity. Driver faults, failed encoder reads, and overspeed conditions feed into motor supervision.

Programmable feedback

Profile data sets detent spacing, strength, damping, and travel limits. The same motor can act like a click wheel, a spring, or a viscous dial, with tap and buzz effects layered on top.

How profiles stay stable

Hysteresis controls when a detent advances, and a small dead zone reduces buzzing near its center. Profiles reach the control task through a mailbox and take effect without putting Bluetooth or application logic in the motor loop.

Knob ↔ computer

Bluetooth HID provides standalone volume and brightness keys. The macOS companion adds two-way setting synchronization and returns control to the knob when its connection ends.

Companion architecture

The SwiftUI app separates Bluetooth transport, protocol decoding, host-setting adapters, and UI state. A verified connection and renewable control lease coordinate which side owns a setting, with simulated transport and host-side tests for the shared logic.
